Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This policy explains how Twonder, developed by Arthur Yessayan, handles information in its iPhone, iPad, and Mac app and on this support website.

Twonder's gameplay runs on your device. The app has no account, developer-operated game server, advertising SDK, or in-app analytics service. It does not send your game progress to the developer. Apple handles App Store purchases. GitHub hosts these support pages.

Game information stored on your device

Twonder stores the current board, random-generator state, score, best score, coin balance, tile records, milestone rewards, sound preference, Premium status, and identifiers used to recognize delivered purchases. This information supports saved games and purchase delivery and is held in the app's local storage. The app does not upload it to a developer-operated service.

Twonder does not provide its own cross-device synchronization. Your device or computer backup may include app data, depending on your Apple and backup settings. Those backups are governed by the service you use.

Purchases through Apple

If you make an in-app purchase or restore Premium, Apple's StoreKit service processes that request. The app receives purchase-related information such as the product identifier, transaction identifier, and purchase status to grant coins or enable Premium and avoid duplicate delivery.

The app does not receive your credit-card number or Apple Account password. Apple handles payment information, purchase history, refunds, and its own data processing under the Apple Privacy Policy. Purchases require access to Apple's services.

Analytics, advertising, and permissions

The current app contains no advertising SDK, developer analytics SDK, or developer-operated crash-reporting service. It does not track you across other apps or websites or sell your personal information. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location access.

Apple may collect App Store or operating-system diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple's policies. This policy does not change those platform settings.

Support information you choose to share

If you open a support issue on GitHub, the developer can read the information you submit, including your GitHub username, message, and attachments. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot, and improve Twonder. Issues and comments are public and are handled through GitHub's service.

Do not post passwords, Apple Account details, payment details, receipts, or other sensitive information. Include only the device/app details needed to describe the problem. Support discussions may remain available while needed to resolve an issue and maintain its history. To request removal of information from a developer-controlled issue, contact us through the support tracker without repeating the sensitive information. GitHub's own retention, public copies, and account data are subject to its policies.

This website and GitHub Pages

These pages use no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, embedded third-party media, or externally loaded fonts. We do not add cookies or a contact form to this website.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in. For hosting and GitHub support-account information, see GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Following external links takes you to services governed by their own policies.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local game information remains until the app's data is removed or overwritten. Restart resets the current run, not the whole player profile. To remove all local app information, remove the app and its associated data using your operating system's controls. On iOS and iPadOS, offloading an app is different from deleting its data. On Mac, removing the application alone may leave its stored data behind. Backups may retain copies under your backup settings.

Removing local data may permanently remove game progress and unused consumable coins. It does not delete Apple's purchase records or automatically refund purchases. Premium can be restored through Apple using the purchasing account; consumable coin packs are not restored through Restore Purchases. There is no developer-held gameplay account to delete.

You can choose not to make purchases or submit support information and continue using core gameplay. Requests concerning information held by Apple or GitHub should be directed to those providers using their privacy processes.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information through gameplay. Purchases are subject to Apple's account and parental controls. Anyone seeking support should avoid posting a child's personal information.

Changes to this policy

We will publish changes on this page with a revised effective date. If future app versions introduce services that change these practices, this policy and the relevant store declarations will be updated.

Contact

For questions about Twonder or this policy, use the Twonder support page and its GitHub support tracker. Public reports should contain no sensitive personal information.
