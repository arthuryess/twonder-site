A little match.
A bigger number.

Twonder is a colorful number puzzle for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Tap connected matching tiles, plan your next move, and work toward 2048 and beyond—at your own pace.

Need help?

Report a problem or ask a question through the public Twonder support tracker on GitHub.

Contact support

GitHub sign-in is required to post. Reports are public. Please do not include your Apple Account details, passwords, payment information, receipts, or other sensitive information.

How to play

Tap a group of equal numbers that touch horizontally, vertically, or diagonally. They merge into a bigger tile at the position you choose, then new tiles fall into the gaps. There is no countdown.

Swap exchanges two selected tiles anywhere on the board. Hammer removes one tile. Each tool costs 50 in-game coins. Cancel a tool before using it to keep your coins.

Common questions

Can I play offline?

Yes. Core gameplay works offline. Purchases, restoring Premium, and visiting these support pages need an internet connection.

Where is my progress saved?

Your board, score, coin balance, records, and settings are stored on your device. Twonder does not provide an account or its own cloud-sync service. Restart begins a new board and score while keeping your coins, records, and settings. Deleting app data may remove locally stored progress and coins.

How do I restore Premium?

Open the gear button, then choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase and an internet connection. Consumable coin packs are not restored through Restore Purchases.

A purchase or coin balance looks wrong. What should I do?

Avoid deleting the app or clearing its data while a purchase issue is unresolved. Check your connection and report the issue with the product name and a description of what happened. Keep transaction details private. Apple's purchase history and refund options are available through Apple's purchase support.

How can I turn off sounds?

Open the gear button and turn Sound off.

What should a bug report include?

Tell us the device model, operating-system version, app version, steps to reproduce the issue, and what you expected. If you attach a screenshot, remove personal information first.
